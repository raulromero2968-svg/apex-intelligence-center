## Summary: Ethical Fusion Architecture

The hybrid Epicurean-Aristotelian system provides:

1. **Epicurean Layer**: Pleasure-pain calculus for immediate hedonic balance
   - Friendship for social bonds
   - Self-sufficiency for internal resilience

2. **Aristotelian Layer**: Golden mean moderation for sustainable flourishing
   - Phronesis for contextual wisdom
   - Temperance for balanced responses
   - Courage for virtuous action

3. **Fusion Mechanism**: RRF-style ranking that queries Epicurean pleasure metrics against Aristotelian means, selecting the action that maximizes eudaimonic gain while minimizing vice (excess/deficiency).

**Production Deployment**: Execute one practice from each layer daily, log metrics, and audit weekly for eudaimonic drift. Rollback to previous virtue caches if net balance drops below thresholds.
