📚 Understanding Portfolio Diversification

Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT) teaches us not to put all our eggs in one basket. The same principle applies to TCG collecting. A well-diversified collection reduces risk and maximizes long-term value.

Risk Score Explained:

  • 10 = Perfectly Diversified: Balanced across multiple TCGs, types, and sets
  • 7-9 = Well Diversified: Good balance with minor concentration
  • 4-6 = Moderate Risk: Some concentration in specific areas
  • 1-3 = High Risk: Heavily concentrated in one TCG, set, or card

Why Diversification Matters:

  • Game Risk: If one TCG loses popularity (e.g., Yu-Gi-Oh! in 2010s), you're not wiped out
  • Reprint Risk: If Pokemon reprints a chase card, your portfolio doesn't tank
  • Market Cycles: Different TCGs peak at different times
  • Liquidity: Diversified portfolios are easier to sell in parts

Ideal Portfolio Allocation:

  • By TCG: No single TCG should exceed 50% of portfolio value
  • By Type: Mix of singles (60%), sealed (30%), graded (10%)
  • By Era: Blend of vintage (40%) and modern (60%)
  • By Card: No single card should exceed 20% of portfolio

Concentration vs. Conviction:

High concentration isn't always bad. If you're a Pokemon expert with deep market knowledge, concentrating in Pokemon can be justified. The key is intentional concentration based on expertise, not accidental concentration from impulse buying.
