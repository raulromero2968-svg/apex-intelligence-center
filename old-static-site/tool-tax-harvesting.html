📚 How Tax Loss Harvesting Works

Tax loss harvesting is a strategy to offset capital gains by selling assets that have declined in value. For TCG collectors with significant portfolios, this can result in substantial tax savings.

Key Concepts:

  • Capital Loss: When you sell a card for less than you paid, you realize a capital loss
  • Tax Deduction: Capital losses can offset capital gains dollar-for-dollar
  • Ordinary Income Offset: Up to $3,000 in losses can offset ordinary income per year
  • Loss Carryforward: Unused losses can be carried forward to future tax years

Holding Periods:

  • Short-term: Held 1 year or less - Taxed at ordinary income rates (10%-37%)
  • Long-term: Held more than 1 year - Taxed at lower capital gains rates (0%-20%)

The Wash Sale Rule:

The IRS "wash sale" rule prohibits claiming a loss if you buy the same or substantially identical asset within 30 days before or after the sale. For TCG cards, this means:

  • Don't sell a Charizard for a loss and buy another Charizard within 30 days
  • Wait at least 31 days before repurchasing
  • Consider buying a different card/set to maintain TCG exposure

Year-End Strategy:

December is prime time for tax loss harvesting. Review your portfolio before December 31st to:

  • Identify cards trading below your purchase price
  • Calculate the tax benefit of selling
  • Execute sales before year-end to claim losses on this year's return
  • Plan repurchases (after 30 days) if you want to maintain position

Example:

You bought a Pokemon PSA 10 Charizard for $5,000, and it's now worth $3,500. If you sell it, you realize a $1,500 loss. In the 24% tax bracket, this saves you $360 in federal taxes. If you also have $4,000 in gains from other cards, this $1,500 loss reduces your taxable gains to $2,500.
