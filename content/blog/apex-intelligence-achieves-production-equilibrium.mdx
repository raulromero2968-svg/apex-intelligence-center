## The Six Immortal Guardrails

1. **Autonomous Schema Sync** – Every Prisma diff is automatically linted, replayed, and versioned before hitting `main`, preventing drift between staging and prod.
2. **Barrel Lockdown Protocol** – Shared exports are generated and frozen per commit so rogue imports cannot bypass the verified surface.
3. **Sentry Singularity** – Release health, tracing, and alert routing are scripted into the deploy pipeline; the failure budget is enforced by code, not hope.
4. **Vercel Binary Convergence** – Preview and production builds now share the same artifact hash, eliminating “works-in-preview” ghosts.
5. **Immutable Asset Ledger** – Public assets (badges, screenshots, proofs) are checksumed and promoted via a notarized manifest.
6. **Manual Override Gate** – Human reviewers can still intervene, but the pipeline logs every touch and revalidates all signatures afterward.

Each guardrail is encoded in our ops repo and referenced in [`VICTORY.md`](/VICTORY.md) with cross-links to [`ETERNITY.md`](/ETERNITY.md) for the long arc of compliance.
