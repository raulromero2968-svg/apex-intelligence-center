# Grading Timing: When to Submit vs When to Wait

Grading service backlogs and turnaround times significantly impact the economics of card grading. This article helps you understand when grading adds real value versus when it's just a waiting game.

## Understanding Grading Economics

Grading isn't always the right choice. The decision depends on multiple factors: the card's raw value, potential grade, grading costs, turnaround time, and market conditions. When these factors align, grading can significantly increase a card's value. When they don't, you're better off waiting or selling raw.

## PSA Backlog Dynamics

PSA backlogs fluctuate based on submission volume, service tier, and seasonal factors. During peak periods, turnaround times can extend to several months, tying up capital and delaying potential sales. Understanding these cycles helps you time submissions for optimal results.

## When Grading Adds Value

Grading typically adds the most value for cards that:
- Have high raw values where grade premiums are significant
- Are likely to receive high grades (PSA 9 or 10)
- Are in high demand in the graded market
- Have sufficient price spread between raw and graded versions

## When to Wait

Sometimes waiting makes more sense. If backlogs are long, market conditions are uncertain, or the card's value is relatively low, holding off on grading can preserve capital and flexibility. Additionally, if you're uncertain about a card's potential grade, getting a pre-screen or second opinion can save time and money.

## Strategic Submission Timing

The best time to submit for grading often aligns with lower submission volumes, when turnaround times are faster. Additionally, submitting before major market events or releases can help ensure your graded cards are ready when demand peaks.
