# Sealed Product Analyzer: Deep Dive into Investment Analysis

Sealed product investing offers unique opportunities and challenges compared to individual cards. The Apex Intelligence sealed analyzer provides comprehensive data and insights to help you evaluate potential investments and track existing positions.

## Understanding Sealed Product Dynamics

Sealed product valuation depends on multiple factors:
- **Print run size and distribution**: Limited print runs typically appreciate faster
- **Set composition**: Chase cards and overall set value drive sealed demand
- **Market liquidity**: Popular sets maintain better liquidity over time
- **Storage and condition**: Sealed products require careful storage to maintain value

## Using the Sealed Analyzer

The analyzer provides detailed insights for sealed product evaluation:
- Historical price tracking for different sealed formats (booster boxes, ETBs, etc.)
- Expected value (EV) calculations based on current single card prices
- Supply and demand indicators from marketplace data
- Comparative analysis across different sets and TCGs

## Investment Evaluation Framework

When evaluating sealed product investments, consider:
- **Risk-adjusted returns**: Compare potential returns against holding periods and risks
- **Storage costs**: Factor in climate-controlled storage and insurance
- **Exit strategy**: Understand when and how you'll sell
- **Market timing**: Track print run cycles and reprint risks
