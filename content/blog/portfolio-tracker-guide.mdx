# Portfolio Tracker Guide: Mastering TCG Investment Tracking

Effective portfolio tracking is essential for successful TCG investing. The Apex Intelligence portfolio tracker provides real-time insights into your collection's performance, helping you make data-driven decisions and optimize your investment strategy.

## Getting Started with Portfolio Tracking

Setting up your portfolio tracker is straightforward:
- Import your existing collection data
- Configure tracking preferences for different card types
- Set up automated price updates for real-time valuations
- Establish performance benchmarks and goals

## Key Metrics to Monitor

The portfolio tracker provides several critical metrics:
- **Total portfolio value**: Real-time valuation based on current market prices
- **Performance attribution**: Understand which cards and categories drive returns
- **Liquidity analysis**: Track how quickly you could convert positions to cash
- **Risk metrics**: Monitor concentration risk and volatility

## Optimization Strategies

Use portfolio insights to optimize your collection:
- Identify underperforming assets that may need to be sold
- Spot opportunities for rebalancing across different TCGs or card types
- Track grading ROI to determine which raw cards are worth submitting
- Monitor market trends to time entry and exit points

## Advanced Features

Advanced portfolio tracking capabilities include:
- Historical performance tracking and trend analysis
- Automated alerts for significant price movements
- Tax reporting and capital gains tracking
- Integration with marketplace data for accurate valuations
