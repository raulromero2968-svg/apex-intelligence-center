# Sealed vs Singles: 2025 Portfolio Split Guide

Building a TCG investment portfolio requires deciding how to allocate between sealed product and individual cards. Both have distinct advantages and trade-offs that vary based on your goals, timeline, and risk tolerance.

## Understanding the Trade-offs

Sealed product and singles serve different purposes in a portfolio. Sealed product offers diversification within a set, protection from condition issues, and potential for unopened premium. Singles offer more targeted exposure, easier liquidity, and the ability to focus on specific high-value cards.

## Liquidity Considerations

Liquidity is a crucial factor. Singles generally sell faster and with less friction—you can list a card and often sell it within days. Sealed product can take longer to sell, especially for less popular sets, but may command premium prices when demand is high.

## Storage and Maintenance Costs

Storage requirements differ significantly. Sealed product takes more space and requires careful climate control to prevent damage. Singles, especially graded cards, are more compact but still need proper storage. Factor in insurance, storage space costs, and maintenance when deciding your allocation.

## Return Profile Analysis

Historical data shows that both sealed and singles can generate strong returns, but the patterns differ. Sealed product often has more predictable, steady appreciation, while singles can have more volatile but potentially higher returns for the right cards.

## Finding Your Sweet Spot

The optimal split depends on your specific situation. Consider your investment timeline, risk tolerance, available capital, storage capacity, and personal preferences. Many successful collectors use a balanced approach, maintaining both sealed product for stability and singles for targeted growth opportunities.
