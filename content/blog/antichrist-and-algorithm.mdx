## [Strand A: The Analysis]

In October 2025, the *Washington Post* leaked recordings of Peter Thiel describing critics of AI regulation as "legionnaires of the Antichrist." This was not a slip of the tongue; it was a strategic deployment of theology to defend a specific economic future: **Rentism**.

In the "Rentism" scenario (one of four possible futures for a post-capitalist world), elites control the infrastructure of abundance - the AI, the algorithms, the energy - and extract value through strict intellectual property regimes. The only threat to this model is democratic regulation.

By framing regulation not as "inefficient" but as *apocalyptic*, Thiel attempts to grant the tech elite **Civilizational Immunity**. He argues that slowing down is "far worse" than any consequences of acceleration. It is a demand for absolute power, justified by a self-appointed messianic status.

The pattern is not new. The **Seven Mountains Framework** from dominionist theology describes seven spheres of cultural influence that must be controlled: Religion, Family, Education, Government, Media, Arts, and Business. Whether or not Thiel subscribes to this theology explicitly, the *structure* of tech elite power mapping follows the same pattern - operating across multiple mountains simultaneously to shape civilizational outcomes.
