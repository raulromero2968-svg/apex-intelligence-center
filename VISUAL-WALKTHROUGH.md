- **Text**: White (#FFFFFF), Gray shades

- **Cards**: Cyber Gray (#1A1F3A)

- **Headings**: Orbitron (weights: 700-900)

- **Sizes**: 4xl-7xl for heroes, xl-3xl for sections, base-lg for body
