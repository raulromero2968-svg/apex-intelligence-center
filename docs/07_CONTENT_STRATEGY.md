# 07: Content Strategy

## Editorial Philosophy: "The Lab & The Arcade"
We splice the rigor of a scientific journal with the energy of a gaming magazine.

### Strand A: The Institute ("The Journal")
* **Benchmarks:** *Nature, Science, Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists.*
* **Tone:** Clinical, forensic, existential, rigorous.
* **Methodology:** We treat market manipulation and systemic power not as "stories" but as **physics**. We use hypothesis testing, data modeling, and peer review.
* **Visuals:** Complex data visualizations, "Doomsday Clocks," systemic flowcharts (Style: *The Economist* meets *MIT Technology Review*).

### Strand B: The Expedition ("The Magazine")
* **Benchmarks:** *National Geographic, Polygon, Kotaku.*
* **Tone:** Adventurous, voice-y, visually immersive.
* **Methodology:** We are explorers mapping a new world. We use "Gaming" metaphors to explain complex economics (e.g., "The Meta," "Nerfing," "Whales").
* **Visuals:** High-fidelity photography, "Cyberpunk Noir" art, "Map of the Day" (Style: *NatGeo* meets *Cyberpunk 2077*).

## Content Types

| Type | Frequency | Style Benchmark | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Scientific Paper** | Quarterly | *Nature* | Primary research on systemic risks (e.g., "Quantifying the Rentist Trajectory"). |
| **The Threat Assessment** | Monthly | *Bulletin of Atomic Scientists* | A "Doomsday Clock" update for the TCG Economy and Global Power structures. |
| **The Expedition Log** | Bi-Weekly | *National Geographic* | Photo-essays and "Ghost Node" maps visualizing the hidden networks of power. |
| **The Patch Notes** | Weekly | *Kotaku* | Fast, punchy updates on market changes and platform features. |
