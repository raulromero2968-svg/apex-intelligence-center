## Phase 1: Next 30 Days — Validation & Narrative Lock-in

**Goal:** Confirm positioning resonates and sharpen the offer.

### 1.1 User Language Interviews (10–15 Calls)

**Target Personas:**
- 4 Freelancers (copywriters, designers, developers)
- 4 Curators (X power users, newsletter writers, researchers)
- 4 Analysts/Educators (consultants, course creators, subject matter experts)

**Interview Questions:**

| Category | Questions |
|----------|-----------|
| **Pain Discovery** | "What's your biggest worry about AI and your income?" |
| | "Have you noticed any changes in client behavior or rates in the past year?" |
| **Prior Attempts** | "What have you actually tried so far to prepare?" |
| | "Have you used any other platforms or tools for monetizing your expertise?" |
| **Success Criteria** | "What would count as a *win* from a platform like Apex in the first 30 days?" |
| | "What would make you recommend Apex to a colleague?" |

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Create interview script based on above questions
- [ ] Schedule 10-15 calls across persona groups
- [ ] Document exact language used by interviewees
- [ ] Update landing page copy to mirror real phrases from users

### 1.4 Prototype RC Ledger & Rules with Development Team

**RC Rules v1 Specification:**

| Event | RC Earned | Cooldown | Daily Cap |
|-------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Publish intel card | 10–50 RC | 1 per 24h | 100 RC |
| Receive upvote | 1–5 RC | 1 per 60s per user | 50 RC |
| Commons contribution | 5–20 RC | 1 per 24h | 60 RC |
| Comment/review | 1–3 RC | 1 per 5min | 30 RC |
| Profile completion | 20 RC | One-time | — |
| First intel sale | 25 RC | One-time | — |

**RC Uses:**

| Action | RC Cost | Effect |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Boost intel visibility | 10–50 RC | Temporary ranking boost |
| Access premium analytics | 100 RC/month | Detailed performance data |
| Submit governance proposal | 250 RC | Vote on platform changes |
| Unlock beta features | 50–200 RC | Early access to new tools |
| Transfer to another user | Variable | P2P reputation transfer |

**Anti-Gaming Mechanisms:**
- Daily earning caps per category
- Per-action cooldowns
- Contributor level tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum)
- Fraud detection via rate limiting and pattern analysis

## Phase 2: Next 90 Days — Closed Beta & Flywheel

**Goal:** 50–100 high-signal creators using the system; real intel and RC flows.

### 2.1 Closed Beta Cohort (50–100 People)

**Prioritization Criteria:**

| Priority | Profile | Why |
|----------|---------|-----|
| 1 | X threaders with 1K+ followers + engagement | Content + distribution |
| 2 | Freelancers with repeatable playbooks | Proven expertise to package |
| 3 | Analysts with PDF decks/reports | Ready-made intel assets |
| 4 | Educators with course content | Teaching + community building |

**Onboarding Process:**
1. Personal intake call (15 min) to understand their work
2. Concierge help to create first 3 intel cards
3. Access to private Discord channel for feedback
4. Weekly office hours with product team
5. RC bonus for early adopters (50 RC starting balance)

---

### 2.2 Measure 3 Core Behaviors

| Metric | Target (30 days) | Target (90 days) |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| **Intel transformations per user** | 2+ | 5+ |
| **Published intel cards per month** | 1+ | 3+ |
| **RC events (upvotes, comments, etc.)** | 10+ | 50+ |
| **User retention (weekly active)** | 60% | 70% |
| **First USD revenue event** | 20% of users | 50% of users |

**Tracking Infrastructure:**
- [ ] Set up analytics dashboard (Posthog/Mixpanel)
- [ ] Define event tracking for all RC-earning actions
- [ ] Create cohort analysis views
- [ ] Weekly metrics review meetings

---

### 2.3 Ship "Minimum Viable Commons"

**MVP Features:**
- [ ] Curated list of free intel pieces
- [ ] Topic tagging and search
- [ ] Simple upvote/save functionality
- [ ] Creator attribution with links to paid work
- [ ] "Featured" section for high-quality contributions

**Design Principles:**
- Feel like a generous, high-signal library
- NOT a dumping ground or content farm
- Quality over quantity (curated, not algorithmic)
- Clear path from Commons → paid intel

---

### 2.4 Run 1–2 Community Events

**Event 1: "First Intel Tournament"**
- Top 5 intel cards (by RC earned) win bonus RC + early perks
- Duration: 2 weeks
- Judging criteria: quality, originality, community impact
- Prizes: 500 RC + featured placement + early access to features

**Event 2: "Live Intel Teardown"**
- Product team reviews submitted intel cards
- Turn 3-5 examples into "gold standard" templates
- Live on Discord + recorded for async viewing
- Creates reusable onboarding content

## Appendix A: Lead Magnet & Diagnostic Ideas

### Lead Magnet: "AI Disruption Playbook"

**Structure:**
1. **For Freelancers:** How to package completed projects into evergreen assets
2. **For Curators:** Turning X threads and reading into sellable intel
3. **For Analysts:** Converting PDF decks into modular intel cards
4. **For Educators:** Building a Commons presence to drive paid work

Each section ends with a mini Apex walkthrough.

**Distribution:**
- Gated PDF download on landing page
- Email sequence follow-up
- Links to essay + demo

---

### Self-Diagnostic Quiz: "How Exposed Is Your Income to AI?"

**Questions:**
1. What percentage of your income comes from a single employer/client?
2. How much of your work could a junior + ChatGPT replicate?
3. Do you have reusable assets (templates, playbooks) from past work?
4. Is your reputation portable beyond one platform?
5. Have you noticed rate pressure or slower leads?

**Output:**
- Risk score (Low/Medium/High)
- 3–5 tailored recommendations
- One recommendation: "Start assetizing your work; here's how Apex does that."
