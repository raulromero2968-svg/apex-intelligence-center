# 01: Company Vision & Mission

## Vision
To become the world's premier **Civilizational Analytics Engine**.
We combine the investigative rigor of *The New York Times* with the cultural pulse of *Polygon*, empowering users to map value, scarcity, and power—from the TCG sandbox to the global stage.

## Mission
**To make power visible and intelligible.**
We produce scientific-grade research on market manipulation and systemic risks, delivered with the narrative flair of top-tier gaming journalism. We democratize access to institutional intelligence, ensuring that deep analysis is not just accurate, but accessible and compelling.

## Core Values

| Value | Description |
|---|---|
| **The Double Helix** | **Rigor + Voice.** Our research (Strand A) must hold up in a court of law; our storytelling (Strand B) must belong in a magazine. We refuse to be boring to be taken seriously. |
| **The Luminous Jellyfish** | **Hope is a discipline.** We map the Abyss (corruption) but always illuminate the Exit (solutions). We fight cynicism with agency. |
| **Truth Tier Logic** | **Show your work.** We distinguish between Adjudicated Fact and Speculative Rumor. If we report it, we can prove it. |
| **Systemic Literacy** | **It's all one game.** We treat TCGs, Economics, and Politics as interconnected systems. A change in interest rates affects a Charizard; a shift in culture affects the S&P 500. |
| **Community-First** | **For the Player and the Analyst.** We serve the System Analyst and the Gamer. We speak their language, respect their intelligence, and protect their data. |
