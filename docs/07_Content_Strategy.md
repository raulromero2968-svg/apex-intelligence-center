# 07: Content Strategy

## Editorial Philosophy: "Scientific Rigor, Gaming Flair"
We reject the false choice between "Academic" (dry) and "Clickbait" (shallow).
Our content model is **The Double Helix**:

### Strand A: The Research ("The Journal")
* **Benchmark:** *The New York Times, The Atlantic, The Economist.*
* **Tone:** Authoritative, forensic, data-heavy.
* **Format:** Deep-dive reports, white papers, verified data visualizations.
* **Goal:** To be cited by courts, academics, and policymakers.

### Strand B: The Culture ("The Magazine")
* **Benchmark:** *Polygon, Kotaku, The Verge.*
* **Tone:** Voice-y, opinionated, energetic, visually stunning.
* **Format:** Newsletters, blog posts, video essays, "Investigator Notebook" excerpts.
* **Goal:** To be shared on Discord, Reddit, and X; to build a loyal fanbase.

## Content Types

| Type | Frequency | Style | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Apex Report** | Quarterly | *The Atlantic* | Deep-dive investigative journalism on systemic issues (e.g., "The Theology of Rentism"). |
| **The Daily Signal** | Daily | *Polygon* | Short, punchy updates on market movements, "Ghost Node" alerts, and "Patch Notes" for the economy. |
| **The Investigator's Log** | Weekly | *Substack* | Personal, Socratic reflections on the research process (Strand B). |
| **The Luminous Letter** | Bi-Weekly | *Kotaku* | Community highlights, "Green Node" solution spotlights, and wins against the machine. |
