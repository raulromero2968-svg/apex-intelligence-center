We build the "Bloomberg Terminal for Truth," using TCG markets as a sandbox to map value, scarcity, and systemic manipulation across all asset classes.

## Mission Statement

To create open-source intelligence tools that enable anyone to:

1. **Trace Provenance** - Follow the chain of custody for any asset, from Pokémon cards to political donations
2. **Map Influence** - Visualize how power flows through institutions using the Seven Mountains framework
3. **Verify Truth** - Apply the Truth Tier system (CONFIRMED → SPECULATIVE) to distinguish fact from narrative
4. **Find Exits** - Identify solution pathways, not just problems (The Luminous Jellyfish Principle)

## Core Values

### 1. The Luminous Jellyfish Principle

> "We do not just map the Abyss (corruption/loss); we map the Exit (solutions/hope). Every analysis must contain a path to agency."

Research without actionable insight is paralysis. Every investigation must answer: "What can be done?" Light persists in the depths.

### 2. The Double Helix Protocol

We never publish "Polished Research" (Strand A) without the "Raw Process" (Strand B). We use AI not to replace human thought, but to *document* the struggle of human thought.

- **Strand A**: The final insight, verified and sourced
- **Strand B**: The messy investigation, false leads included, showing our work

### 3. Truth Tier Integrity

A database constraint that forces us to categorize data confidence:

| Tier | Definition | Color |
|------|------------|-------|
| `CONFIRMED` | Court documents, official records, direct evidence | Green |
| `DOCUMENTED` | Credible journalism, multiple independent sources | Blue |
| `ALLEGED` | Single source, unverified but plausible | Yellow |
| `SPECULATIVE` | Pattern-based inference, requires more evidence | Red |

We prioritize *accuracy* over *coverage*. Better to say "we don't know" than to amplify noise.

### 4. Radical Traceability

"Show me the source, not just the summary."

Every claim in our system links back to primary sources. Users can always drill down to the original document, filing, or testimony.
