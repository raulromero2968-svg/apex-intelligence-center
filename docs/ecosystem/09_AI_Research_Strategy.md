# 09: AI Research & Strategy

## Core AI Strategy
Our AI strategy is built on the **Double Helix Protocol**: Intelligence is not just the answer; it is the *traceability* of the answer.

## Key Research Pillars

### 1. The Truth Tier Architecture
**Concept:** We reject "Hallucination" by enforcing strict database constraints.
**Application:** Our Graph Engine categorizes all relationships by confidence:
* **Adjudicated:** Court records, verified filings.
* **Documented:** Corporate records, primary sources.
* **Circumstantial:** Flight logs, photo evidence.
* **Speculative:** Unverified rumors (flagged for review).

### 2. Civilizational Analytics (Seven Mountains)
**Concept:** We use large language models (LLMs) to map power dynamics across seven universal domains: *Religion, Family, Education, Government, Media, Arts, Business.*
**Application:** Our "Manus" agent scans unstructured text to identify cross-domain influence (e.g., a Tech CEO influencing Religious discourse).

### 3. The Luminous Jellyfish (Solution Mapping)
**Concept:** AI usually optimizes for engagement (doom-scrolling). We optimize for **Agency**.
**Application:** For every "Problem Cluster" identified (e.g., Cobalt Mining), the AI actively scans for and links "Solution Nodes" (e.g., Catch-Up Clubs), ensuring the user is never left in the Abyss.
