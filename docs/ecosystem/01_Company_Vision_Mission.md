# 01: Company Vision & Mission

## Vision
To make power visible. We are building the **Bloomberg Terminal for Truth**, empowering individuals to map value, scarcity, and systemic manipulation—whether in the TCG market or the architecture of global power.

## Mission
Our mission is to democratize access to **Civilizational Analytics**.
We use Trading Card Games (TCGs) as a sandbox to train our systems on market manipulation, wash trading, and artificial scarcity. We then apply those same high-resolution lenses to the macrocosm: global elite networks, rentist economic structures, and the supply chains of technology.

## Core Values

| Value | Description |
|---|---|
| **The Double Helix** | We never present the "Polished Result" (Strand A) without the "Raw Process" (Strand B). True intelligence requires showing the messy, human struggle for truth alongside the data. |
| **The Luminous Jellyfish** | **Hope is a discipline.** We do not just map the Abyss (corruption, exploitation); we map the Exit (solutions, resistance). Every analysis must contain a path to agency. |
| **Truth Tier Logic** | We prioritize accuracy over coverage. We distinguish between **Adjudicated Fact** and **Speculative Rumor**. If we cannot verify it, we flag it. |
| **Data-Driven Sovereignty** | We believe users should own their data and their insights. We provide the tools (Investigator Notebooks, Local-First Storage) to protect independent thought. |
| **Community-Focused** | We are built for the **System Analyst**—the collector, the researcher, and the citizen who refuses to be manipulated by black-box algorithms. |
