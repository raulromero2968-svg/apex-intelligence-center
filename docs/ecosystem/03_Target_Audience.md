# 03: Target Audience

## Primary Audience: The System Analyst
**Who they are:**
* Researchers, Investigative Journalists, "Autistic-Coded" Pattern Matchers.
* Disillusioned by mainstream narratives ("The Nothing Burger").
* Deeply concerned about the "Abyss" (Trafficking, Corruption) but seeking constructive tools.

**Psychographics:**
* **Forensic Mindset:** Values verifiable data over emotional appeals.
* **Privacy-Conscious:** Distrusts cloud storage; prefers local tools.
* **Agency-Driven:** Wants to *do* something about the problems they see.

## Secondary Audience: The TCG Investor
**Who they are:**
* High-net-worth collectors who understand that the card market is a rigged game.
* They use Apex not just to track prices, but to track *manipulation*.

## User Personas

### Persona 1: "Cipher" (The Analyst)
* **Role:** Independent Researcher.
* **Quote:** "I don't want your opinion. I want the raw flight logs and the connection map."
* **Needs:** The Truth Terminal, Ghost Node visualization, Investigator Notebook.

### Persona 2: "Whale" (The Investor)
* **Role:** TCG Portfolio Manager.
* **Quote:** "If the market is moving, I want to know who is pulling the strings."
* **Needs:** Real-time pricing, Wash-trading detection, Provenance scanning.
