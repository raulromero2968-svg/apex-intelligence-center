## Section 1: What RC Is (And Isn't)

### Heading
**RC is proof of contribution**

### Body Copy

Reputation Credits (RC) measure what you've contributed to the Apex ecosystem over time.

When you publish valuable intel, help other users, participate in governance, or improve the Commons, you earn RC. The more you contribute, the more RC you accumulate.

### What RC Does

| RC Unlocks | Examples |
|------------|----------|
| **Roles** | Curator, Moderator, Governor |
| **Features** | Advanced analytics, curation tools |
| **Visibility** | Boost your intel in search |
| **Discounts** | Lower marketplace fees |
| **Governance** | Vote on platform decisions |

### What RC Does NOT Do

- **RC cannot be bought.** No amount of money gets you RC.
- **RC cannot be traded.** There's no marketplace for reputation.
- **RC cannot be cashed out.** It's not a currency. It's a signal.

### Callout Box
> **Why does this matter?**
>
> Unlike platforms that let you buy credibility, Apex reputation is time-locked. You can't shortcut your way to trust. Someone with 5,000 RC has demonstrated sustained, valuable contribution. That's something money can't fake.

## Section 2: How You Earn RC

### Heading
**Earn RC by contributing value**

### Body Copy

Every RC you earn is tied to a specific action. Here's how contributions translate to RC:

### Earning Table

#### Publishing Intel

| Action | RC Earned | Limits |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Publish your first intel card | +100 | Once ever |
| Publish an intel card | +5 | Per card |
| Your intel gets upvoted | +1 | Max 50 per card |
| Your intel gets bookmarked | +2 | Max 40 per card |
| Someone purchases your intel | +10 | Per sale |
| Receive a 5-star rating | +15 | Per rating |

#### Contributing to Commons

| Action | RC Earned | Limits |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Publish free resource to Commons | +10 | Per resource |
| Your Commons content gets upvoted | +1 | Max 30 per item |
| Your Commons content gets downloaded | +1 | Per 100 downloads |

#### Helping Others

| Action | RC Earned | Limits |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Your comment marked "helpful" | +1 to +3 | Based on impact |
| Valid spam/abuse report | +2 | Max 10/month |

#### Governance

| Action | RC Earned | Limits |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Vote on a governance proposal | +1 | Per vote |
| Voted with majority on passed proposal | +25 | Per proposal |

### Daily Limits

To prevent gaming, RC earning has daily caps:

| Category | Daily Cap |
|----------|-----------|
| Passive events (upvotes, bookmarks) | 50 RC |
| All sources combined | 100 RC |

### Callout Box
> **Why daily limits?**
>
> We want consistent contribution, not overnight farming. Someone who contributes steadily over months is more valuable than someone who tries to game 1,000 RC in a weekend.

## Section 3: How You Use RC

### Heading
**RC unlocks access based on your track record**

### Body Copy

RC isn't spent like currency. It mostly works as **thresholds**--once you reach a level, you keep the benefits as long as your RC stays above it.

### Threshold Unlocks

| RC Level | What You Unlock |
|----------|-----------------|
| **100 RC** | Verified Creator badge |
| **300 RC** | Curator role: create lists, featured nominations |
| **500 RC** | Creator+ Dashboard: advanced analytics, revenue insights |
| **1,000 RC** | Moderator eligibility + 5% fee discount |
| **2,500 RC** | Governor tier: submit top-level proposals, weighted votes |
| **5,000 RC** | 10% marketplace fee discount |
| **10,000 RC** | 15% marketplace fee discount |

### Spending RC (Optional)

A few actions let you **stake** or **spend** RC:

| Action | RC Cost | What Happens |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Boost intel visibility | 20 RC (staked) | 7-day visibility boost. RC returned minus 2 RC fee. |
| Submit governance proposal | 100 RC (staked) | If proposal passes: RC returned. If it fails: partial to full loss. |

### Callout Box
> **Staking vs. Spending**
>
> When you stake RC, you're putting it at risk. If your action succeeds (proposal passes, boost ends), you get most or all of it back. This prevents spam while still letting you use your reputation for impact.

## Section 4: How You Track Your RC

### Heading
**Complete transparency on every RC change**

### Body Copy

Every time your RC changes, you'll see exactly why.

### Your RC Dashboard Shows

- **Current balance** - Your total RC right now
- **Lifetime earned** - All RC you've ever earned
- **Level progress** - How close you are to the next tier
- **Daily remaining** - How much more you can earn today

### Transaction History

Every RC event is logged with:

- What happened (e.g., "Intel upvoted")
- How much RC changed (+1, -20, etc.)
- When it happened
- Link to the source (the intel, comment, or vote)

### Example Transaction Log

```
+5 RC    Published "AI Tools for Researchers"     2 hours ago
+1 RC    Intel upvoted (user_abc)                 3 hours ago
+2 RC    Intel bookmarked (user_xyz)              5 hours ago
-20 RC   Staked for visibility boost              1 day ago
+100 RC  First intel published (bonus)            3 days ago
```

## Section 6: Safeguards & Fair Play

### Heading
**We detect and prevent gaming**

### Body Copy

RC only means something if it can't be faked. Here's how we protect the system:

### Anti-Abuse Measures

| Safeguard | What It Does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Daily caps** | Prevents overnight farming |
| **New account limits** | Accounts < 7 days earn 50% RC |
| **Vote weight** | Votes from accounts < 30 days count less |
| **Voting ring detection** | Flags coordinated voting for review |
| **Report rewards** | Valid spam reports earn RC; false reports don't |

### If Something Goes Wrong

- **Suspicious activity pauses** - If we detect unusual patterns, RC earning pauses while we review
- **RC reversals** - Confirmed fraud results in RC being removed
- **Appeals** - You can always contest moderation decisions

### Callout Box
> **Transparency commitment**
>
> We don't use hidden scores or shadow adjustments. If your RC changes, you'll see why. If we flag your account, you'll know. If we make a rule change, we announce it before it happens.

## Section 7: FAQ

### Heading
**Common Questions**

---

**Can I buy RC?**

No. RC is earned through contribution only. There's no way to purchase RC directly or indirectly.

---

**Can I transfer RC to someone else?**

No. RC is non-transferable. Your reputation is yours alone.

---

**What happens if I stop using Apex?**

Your RC stays with your account. If you come back, it's still there. (Note: In future versions, we may introduce a "rolling 12-month" active RC for governance weight, but lifetime RC will always be preserved for display.)

---

**Can my RC go negative?**

No. Your RC balance never drops below zero. However, penalties for abuse can reduce your balance.

---

**How do I see my transaction history?**

Go to your profile > RC Dashboard > Transaction History. You can filter by date, type, and source.

---

**Why did I stop earning RC today?**

You've likely hit the daily cap (100 RC total, 50 from passive sources). Your earning resets at midnight UTC.

---

**What's the difference between staking and spending?**

Staking puts RC at risk temporarily--you may get it back. Spending (rare in v1) consumes RC permanently.

---

**Will RC ever become a token?**

We have no plans to tokenize RC. If this ever changes, it would require 90+ days notice, a governance vote, and a clear opt-out path. See our User Bill of Rights.
